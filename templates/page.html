This private service stores your iCloud app-specific password, drafts, organizer records and approval payloads encrypted on the VPS. OAuth tokens are stored as hashes. Live mail is fetched on demand. When routine indexing is enabled, a separate encrypted index caches recent message headers and bounded body text on this VPS. Full-text tokens and the index journal are encrypted too; there are no external AI calls or analytics.

Messages you ask ChatGPT to read are sent to ChatGPT. Its account and workspace data controls also apply. Email bodies and attachments can contain misleading instructions; treat them as data.

Your Apple password is never provided to ChatGPT. Owner access requires a separate password and authenticator. OAuth connections use S256 PKCE, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, scoped permissions and revocation.

No system is risk-free. A compromise of root access or the running service can expose mail credentials. Keep SSH secured, apply updates, protect your ChatGPT account and authenticator, and revoke the Apple app-specific password if compromise is suspected.

Live reads are not retained separately. Routine indexing retains up to 90 days in selected folders, with explicit message/body limits. Disconnecting makes the cache unavailable immediately; the next worker run purges it. The rebuildable index is excluded from application backups. ChatGPT drafts are cached encrypted here and synced to iCloud Drafts. Replaced draft versions and submitted drafts are moved to iCloud Trash. Edits made in Apple Mail are detected rather than silently overwritten; this is not a background two-way draft editor. Saved organizer records remain until removed by the owner. Completed proposal content is purged after 30 days and audit metadata after 90 days. Local rules identify possible requests and commitments for review; they do not create confirmed obligations or act on mail. There is no carrier polling, Apple Calendar sync, or automatic notification delivery.
